METHODOLOGY

How Healthy AI works

01 — Dataset HAM10000 dermatoscopic skin-lesion dataset.
02 — Preprocessing Images are resized to 224 × 224 RGB tensors with ImageNet normalization.
03 — Augmentation Flips, rotation, crop and controlled color augmentation are used during training.
04 — Transfer learning MobileNetV2 initialized from ImageNet weights is fine-tuned for the seven HAM10000 classes.
05 — Browser deployment The trained model is exported to ONNX and statically quantized for local browser inference.
IMPORTANT

Educational prototype

Healthy AI is a college machine-learning research project. It classifies images according to patterns learned from the HAM10000 dataset.

The prediction and confidence displayed by this application are model outputs. They are not a medical diagnosis or a clinically validated risk score.

Suspicious, changing, bleeding or otherwise concerning skin lesions should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

🔒 Images are processed locally by the browser model after the model has been installed. The application does not require a cloud prediction API.
